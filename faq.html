Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our printable school projects, science models and habitat dioramas.

What do I receive after purchase?

You receive a digital printable PDF project. Nothing will be shipped to your home. Download the PDF, print the pages and use them to build your project.

How do I get my printable project?

After your payment is completed, the website provides access to your secure PDF download. Save the file to your computer or device so you can print it when you are ready.

Can I print the PDF at home?

Yes. Our projects are designed to work with a regular home or school printer. For sturdier pieces, you can use thicker paper or glue printed pieces onto light cardboard.

Do I need special craft supplies?

No special equipment is required for most projects. You will usually need a printer, paper, scissors and glue. For habitat dioramas, a shoebox or another cardboard box can make a useful base.

Can children build these projects?

Yes. They are designed as hands-on school and home activities. The amount of adult help depends on the child's age and crafting experience. Younger children should have adult supervision when using scissors or other craft tools.

Are these projects useful for school assignments?

They are designed to help students create visual projects about subjects such as the water cycle, animal habitats, ecosystems and the natural world. Always check the teacher's assignment requirements before choosing a project.

Which habitat projects are available?

Our current printable collection includes Ocean, Forest, Rainforest, Desert and Savanna habitat dioramas, plus the Water Cycle science project. More school projects can be added over time.

Can I print the project more than once?

You may print your purchased file as needed for your own personal or household use. The digital PDF itself may not be resold, shared, redistributed or uploaded for others to download.

My download is not working. What should I do?

Visit our Contact & Help page and tell us which project you purchased and what happened. We can then help you with the download problem.

Will I receive a physical diorama?

No. These products are digital downloads only. The photographs show examples of what you can create using the printable project pages.

Still need help?

If you have a question about a purchase, payment, download, printing or building your project, send us a message.
