Rainforest Habitat Diorama for Kids

Create a colorful 3D tropical rainforest filled with lush plants and rainforest animals. Students print, cut and assemble the pieces to build their own habitat diorama.

A hands-on school project for exploring rainforest habitats, animals and ecosystems while making something children can proudly present in class.

See the Project

Build a Tropical Rainforest Habitat

The printable project combines illustrated rainforest backgrounds, plants and separate animal pieces to create a layered 3D scene. Children can decide where the animals belong and arrange the habitat in their own way.

Rainforest AnimalsPrintable animals include an orangutan, sloths, toucan, anteater, capybara, turtle, frog, spotted big cat and other tropical wildlife.
Tropical SceneryDense green backgrounds, trees, hanging vegetation and forest-floor details create the rainforest environment.
3D DioramaFreestanding animals and layered scenery turn the printable pages into a dimensional school display.
Habitat LearningThe finished model can support lessons and discussions about rainforest animals, habitats, ecosystems and biodiversity.

Printable Rainforest Animals

The animal pieces are printed separately so students can cut them out and choose where to place them throughout the rainforest habitat.

Clear Visual Building Instructions

The printable pages use visual placement symbols to help show where the different background and scenery pieces fit when assembling the diorama.

See the Rainforest in 3D

Layered tropical backgrounds and freestanding animals create depth throughout the finished habitat.
