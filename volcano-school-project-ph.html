A Hands-on Volcano Model for Science and Geography Projects

Need a volcano model for a school assignment? The scenery and information cards are already illustrated, giving children a starting point for building, learning and explaining their project.

This printable volcano model has English labels and instructions. Use its four scenes to discuss what happens inside a volcano and how eruptions change the surrounding environment.

Four Scenes, One Volcano Project

Build the four sections and bring them together into a display that children can explain from different sides.

  • Inside a volcano: trace the route from magma to the crater and lava flow.
  • New land from lava: explore how cooled lava can build new land.
  • Rich volcanic soil: discuss how weathered volcanic material can contribute minerals to soil.
  • Eruption hazards: show how lava and other volcanic hazards can affect homes and the environment.

A Volcano Cross Section with Labelled Parts

The numbered cross section and matching English key identify magma, the main vent, throat, crater, lava flow and layers of ash. Children can point to the parts while explaining their model.
