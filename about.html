Hello, I’m Ronald

I design printable projects that turn everyday craft materials into small worlds for children and families to build.

From flat paper to a small world

I enjoy imagining a scene, drawing its pieces and finding out how they fit together. My worktable often holds paper scraps, test pieces and another idea waiting to be built. A design does not always work the first time; adjusting a fold or moving a piece is part of the process.

Over time, that curiosity grew into a collection of printable dioramas, miniature scenes and school projects. I also share my designs through Etsy. The pleasure is still the same: watching a few printed pages become something you can hold.

Made for families who want to create

Making things for and with my children has shaped how I think about these projects. A template should leave room for their ideas. They can move the animals, add scenery or tell a story about the little world they have made.

You do not have to finish a project in one sitting. Print the pages, cut a few pieces together and return to the scene later. Family craft time can be about the small discoveries along the way as much as the finished display.

An easier starting point for busy parents

The printable pieces save you from designing every tree, animal and background yourself. You provide the printer, paper, scissors and glue, plus a box or backing card when the project needs it. Check the project instructions before you begin.

Parents can help with preparation and tricky parts while children make choices, arrange the pieces and explain what they are building. I want the templates to make starting easier while keeping the creative work in your child’s hands.

Science projects and learning at home

School Projects Philippines brings together paper models for families looking for science craft ideas and habitat dioramas. For a classroom performance task, choose a model that fits the teacher’s instructions and let your child prepare their own explanation.

You can also use a habitat diorama or water cycle model for a weekend activity or learning at home. Ask questions, explore the topic together and add your own details. These are independent craft resources rather than an official school course.

What would you like to build?

I hope you find a project that makes you want to clear a space on the table and start creating. For printing and materials, visit the FAQ. If you need help with a project or download, send me a message.
